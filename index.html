Good questions

Quick answers about working with a Denver social media agency for food and drink brands.

What does a social media manager do for a restaurant or food brand?

A social media manager plans, creates, and publishes your content, writes your captions, replies to comments and messages, and reports on what is working. Cherry Salt handles strategy, content planning, copywriting, Instagram Reel and TikTok video creation, scheduling, community engagement, and monthly or quarterly reporting.

Where is Cherry Salt based?

Cherry Salt is based in Denver, Colorado, and works with restaurants, bars, bakeries, breweries, cafés, food trucks, catering companies, ice cream shops, and CPG brands in Boulder, Arvada, Golden, Edgewater, and the greater Denver area.

What kinds of businesses does Cherry Salt work with?

Cherry Salt is a social media agency for food-focused brands: restaurants, catering companies, ice cream shops, breweries, cafés, bars, food trucks, bakeries, and CPG brands.

Which social media platforms does Cherry Salt manage?

Cherry Salt launches and manages accounts on Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, X, and Threads, and can set up Meta Ads Manager.

Can Cherry Salt help if my business is new to social media?

Yes. Cherry Salt can launch your accounts, audit the ones you already have, create a logo and brand guidelines, and shoot photos and video of your food and drinks before ongoing management begins.

How much does social media management cost?

Every package is built around your brand and your budget, so there is no one-size price. Book a free consultation and Cherry Salt will recommend what fits.
